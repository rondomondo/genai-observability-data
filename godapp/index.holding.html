GOD

Genai Observability Data

A synthetic observability data generator that produces realistic distributed traces and structured logs for GenAI-powered systems, following the [OpenTelemetry GenAI Semantic Conventions](https://opentelemetry.io/docs/specs/semconv/gen-ai/) (semconv 1.41.0, experimental). Use it to populate an OTel stack (Jaeger + Loki + Grafana + Prometheus) with representative data for dashboarding, alerting rule development, anomaly detection, or testing observability tooling against GenAI workloads - without needing a live production system.
Systems initialising. Launching soon.
