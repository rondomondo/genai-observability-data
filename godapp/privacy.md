**Last Updated:** 01JUN2026

Because this App uses Google OAuth2 for authentication, we may access certain information from your Google Account based on the specific scopes requested during login. This may include:

* **Basic Profile Information:** Your name, email address, and profile picture (if standard scopes like `profile` or `email` are requested).

We use the collected information strictly for the following purposes:

* **We do not sell, trade, rent, or share your personal information with third parties.**

* **Retention:** We only retain your data for as long as necessary to perform the testing functions of the App.

### 4. User Control and Data Deletion

* **Revoking Access:** You can revoke the App's access to your Google Account at any time through your [Google Account Security Settings](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions).
